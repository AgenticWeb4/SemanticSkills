# Style-Independent Rules

Use `--accent` only for actual emphasis—selected state, risk, conclusion, or decisive number—with at
most six emphasis sites per page. Map imported colors to accent, secondary accent, and neutrals;
preserve chart legends when color encodes data.

Use system sans and mono stacks. Body sizes come from 13, 15, 17, 19, 21, 24, 27, or 30px; titles
are at least 34px and hero numbers at least 64px. Teaching and self-read prose is at least 21px;
report cards may use 18px short phrases. Table cells, axes, diagram labels, and screenshot text may
remain 15px.

Titles are claims naming the relevant technology or method. Prefer concrete verbs, measured numbers
with basis, and plain language. Remove slogans, generic adjectives, invented metaphors, and
page-level interaction instructions.

Page hierarchy is eyebrow, 40–46px title, optional lead, flexible body, and footer. Peer cards with
the same role are fully isomorphic in fill, border, radius, padding, typography, and spacing.
Highlight only when copy explains the difference.

Use alignment, whitespace, and scale before decoration. Keep one dominant idea, one visual grammar,
and a deliberate focal point per page. Verify legibility at presentation scale, not browser zoom.
