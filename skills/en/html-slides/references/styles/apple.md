# Apple Keynote-Inspired Style

Use a dark, minimal, product-centered profile with extreme scale contrast, generous whitespace,
borderless surfaces, and a single gradient or accent moment. This is an experiential style, not an
official Apple template.

Prefer one-point hero pages, centered product imagery, three-column specifications, and sparse
comparison/evidence pages. `.ap-hero` holds one dominant statement, `.ap-grad` is used once for a
keyword or number, `.ap-product` centers an image at up to 70% height, and `.ap-spec` presents large
metrics without card borders.

Do not add a standard footer unless page numbers are requested. Cover and closing are one statement
on dark background. Product images and logos are user-supplied and inlined as data URIs.
