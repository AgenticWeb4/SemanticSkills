# Huawei Official-Deck-Inspired Style

Use Huawei's public-deck visual grammar: white or controlled dark backgrounds, disciplined red
emphasis, strong black titles, geometric corner marks, evidence-heavy cases, and formal footers.
Community-maintained; trademarks remain Huawei's.

Red marks current state, decisive evidence, or key numbers, never every container. Preferred
patterns include chapter openers, 1–2–1 problem/solution/value, AS-IS/TO-BE, KPI bands,
architecture, evidence pairs, and five-part cases.

Available classes: `.hw-corner` for partial red image corners, `.hw-bar` for a small stage label,
`.hw-quote` for attributed evidence, `.hw-conclusion` for one bottom takeaway, `.hw-crumb` for
long-deck navigation, `.hw-qr` for a labeled QR position, and `.hw-underline` for agenda emphasis.

Footer left holds page and selected confidentiality text; footer right uses the horizontal logo.
Light/dark cover KVs and logos live under `assets/huawei/` and must be inlined. A Huawei Cloud
sub-brand footer may use its provided logo. Closing uses “Thank you.” plus approved
mission/copyright text and no standard footer.

Never invent official claims, confidentiality, attribution, or mission wording. Ask for required
legal text and user-supplied sub-brand assets.
